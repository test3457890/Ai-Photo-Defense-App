The photograph is the focus; progressive disclosure.
7. **Three genuinely different modes** sharing engine, projects, settings and editing state without loss on switching. Essential (ImageOptim-like: drag-drop, processing list, simple presets, quick scan, basic crop and redaction, metadata removal, progress, quick and batch export, warnings; no research controls). Professional (Capture One / Lightroom / Affinity-like: large preview, filmstrip, media browser, compact toolbar, adjustable panels, histogram, colour, exposure, layers, masks, object selection, Privacy Scan, before/after, advanced protection, export; panels rearranged/collapsed/resized).
